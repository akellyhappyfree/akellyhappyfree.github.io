Types of Digital Cameras

Camera Type Best For Skill Level Price Key Strength
DSLR Professional photography Intermediate → Pro $450 – $3200+ Optical viewfinder, long battery life
Mirrorless Travel & hybrid shooting Beginner → Pro $600 – $4100+ Compact, fast autofocus
Point-and-Shoot Casual photography Beginner $100 – $600 Simple and portable
Smartphone Everyday use All levels Already owned Convenience and instant sharing
Medium Format Studio & commercial work Professional $3400 – $10000+ Extreme image quality

DSLR Cameras

Known for reliability and image quality. Great for learning manual settings and professional shooting with many attachable lenses.

Mirrorless Cameras

Modern and lightweight. Excellent for video and fast autofocus performance.

Point-and-Shoot

Compact and easy to use. Perfect for beginners or casual photography.

Smartphone Cameras

Mobile and easy to store and use at any time. These cameras are improving to the point of nearly rivaling professional equipment.

Medium Format Cameras

Used by professionals for the highest image quality. Ideal for studio and commercial work.
