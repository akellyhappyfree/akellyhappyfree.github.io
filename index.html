Tips and Tricks

Here are some useful tips for improving your photography skills!

  • Rule of Thirds: Imagine your frame divided into a 3x3 grid. Place key elements along these lines or at their intersections for a more balanced composition.
  • Golden Hour: Shoot during the first hour after sunrise and the last hour before sunset for soft, warm lighting
  • Leading Lines: Use natural lines in your scene (like roads, fences, or rivers) to guide the viewer's eye towards the main subject.
  • Experiment with Perspectives: Change your viewpoint by shooting from high above, down low, or through objects to add interest and uniqueness to your photos.
  • Keep it Steady
    Use a tripod or steady surface to avoid blur.
  • Focus on the Eyes:
    When photographing people or animals, ensure the eyes are in sharp focus to create a stronger connection with the viewer.
